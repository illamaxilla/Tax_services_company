Phase 1

Fragmented citations, no verified Google Business Profile, one review. In 2026, Google Business Profile actions grew 41% year over year while ordinary website traffic declined. More people will judge you from your Google listing than from any other single asset you own, and yours is not competing.

Phase 2

Capture. Today, an interested person either calls or vanishes. We add a landing page built for one action, prominent click-to-call, a short lead form, and text-message capture. Most local service enquiries arrive on a phone, so every capture point is designed for a thumb first.

Qualify. Not every enquiry deserves an hour of your time. A short intake form — five to seven questions, conversational, mobile-first — establishes whether someone is a W-2 filer wanting a quick refund or a contractor with three years of unfiled returns. High-fit enquiries route to you immediately with a summary; low-fit enquiries route to a self-serve path. This is where “high ticket” stops being a hope and becomes a filter.

Convert. A structured consultation, a clear proposal, an engagement letter, a signature. We supply the templates and the sequence; you supply the expertise.

Retain. You are already exceptional here. What is missing is the systematic layer: the review ask at the right moment, the off-season touch, the annual reminder, the referral ask. Automated, in your voice, so it happens whether or not you remember.

06

The reactivation campaign

Singled out because it is the highest-return thing in this document and it requires no advertising.

You have around 300 people who already know you. Some filed this year. Many have not for several years. Every one of them chose you at least once.

Why this works when advertising has not

A stranger must be persuaded that you exist, that you are competent, and that you are trustworthy. Someone on that list has already concluded all three. The only question is timing and offer. The cost difference is not incremental — it is an order of magnitude.

What we build

  1. 01Clean the list. Deduplicate, correct, segment.
  2. 02Segment by recency. Filed this year · filed 1–2 years ago · dormant 3+ years.
  3. 03A distinct message per segment. A client who left three years ago should not receive the same email as one who filed in March.
  4. 04Sequence: value first, offer second. Something genuinely useful before anything is asked for.
  5. 05Multi-channel. Email and text. Text response rates for a known contact are dramatically higher.
  6. 06Track everything. Opens, clicks, replies, bookings, engagements.

The off-season angle

This campaign runs in the months you said you want to be busier. Tax planning, quarterly estimates for contractors, IRS resolution and bookkeeping are all off-season work — and they are the higher-value services you named as most worth your time.

Line 18 of your last schedule listed tax planning at 0% of billing, and Line 19 named it as the most valuable use of your time. Closing that gap is the single clearest revenue opportunity in the business.

What we need. The list, in whatever form it exists — spreadsheet, software export, or paper we can transcribe.

03

Why this matters more in 2026 than it did in 2024

The mechanics of local discovery changed materially in the last eighteen months. Three shifts matter to you.

Shift 1 — AI now answers the question before the click

Google's AI Overviews expanded from informational queries to local intent queries in late 2025. Searches like “tax preparer near me” or “tax help Lake City” now frequently return an AI-written answer above the map results.

The critical detail, and it is good news: the businesses cited in those AI answers are drawn from the businesses already ranking in the traditional map pack. Nothing exotic is required. Profile completeness, category accuracy, review velocity, citation consistency and locally relevant content still decide it.

What has changed is the penalty for neglect. Previously an unoptimised business ranked fourth and got some traffic. Now the query is often answered without them appearing at all.

Shift 2 — The Business Profile is the new homepage

Google's 2026 figures show Business Profile actions — calls, direction requests, website clicks, bookings — grew 41% year over year, while traditional organic traffic to websites declined.

More people will decide about you from your Google listing than from your website. Your listing is now the primary storefront, which is why section 7 is the longest part of this document.

Shift 3 — Assistants are a third discovery channel

ChatGPT, Perplexity and similar tools have become a meaningful source of local business recommendations, behind Google and Facebook. These systems are considerably more selective than search — a high Google ranking does not guarantee a mention.

What they rely on is consistent, structured, verifiable data across multiple independent sources, which is precisely what section 1 shows you do not currently have. Fixing your citations serves traditional search and AI recommendation simultaneously; there is no trade-off.

The one honest caveat. Local and transactional queries show lower AI Overview adoption than informational ones, so the effect on “tax preparer Lake City” is real but less severe than the headline figures suggest. The point is that the same work serves both worlds, so we do it once and properly.

The review engine

Why this outranks advertising in priority

For buyers: a contractor choosing who handles a $4,000 IRS problem reads reviews before calling. One review from 2017 does not survive comparison against a national chain.

For ranking: review count, rating, recency and velocity — the rate at which new reviews arrive — are among the most heavily weighted controllable local ranking factors. Ten reviews arriving over ten weeks is worth considerably more than ten arriving in one afternoon, which also looks manufactured.

Rules

  • Get the Google review short link from the Business Profile dashboard once verified

  • Never offer anything in exchange. Google removes incentivised reviews and it breaches their terms. For a firm holding an IRS PTIN it is not worth the exposure

  • Do not filter or gate. Ask everyone who had a good experience

Value first, ask second. A dormant contact who receives only a request is unlikely to respond and may unsubscribe.

When a negative review arrives

It will, eventually, to every business. What determines the damage is the response, which is read by far more people than the review itself.

  • Respond within 48 hours. A prompt, measured reply reads better than a perfect one three weeks late
  • Never dispute facts publicly, and never mention anything about their return. Confirming that someone is a client and discussing their tax matter in public is a confidentiality problem before it is a marketing one
  • Acknowledge, take it offline, provide a direct contact
  • Do not argue, even when you are right. Prospective clients read the tone, not the merits

Usable template

Thank you for telling us — I'm sorry your experience fell short of what we aim for. I'd like to understand what happened and put it right. Please call me directly on (386) 487-5145.

— Kerceus Andre, Kingdom Tax Services

One negative review among twenty positive ones is not a problem — a flawless five-star record reads as manufactured. A handled complaint demonstrates accountability, which is what someone handing over their finances is looking for.

Two things worth discussing

The credential question

Public directories list you as PTIN — Registered Tax Preparer. At least one Lake City competitor is listed as an Enrolled Agent.

For the individual returns you file now, this is largely invisible. For the higher-value planning and resolution work you said you want, it matters more — an EA holds unlimited representation rights before the IRS, which is directly relevant to resolution cases, and buyers of that work do look.

Not a blocker, and we can market effectively around it by leading on tenure and retention. But it shapes what we can claim without overreaching, and the Annual Filing Season Program is a lighter-weight credential worth knowing about. Better raised now than after a campaign is running.

The “No Upfront Fee” message

It works. It is also why your average return sits between $75 and $375.

That message will need to change if the goal is higher-value clients, and changing it may reduce raw enquiry volume while raising enquiry quality. We should agree that trade-off deliberately rather than discover it in the numbers, because for a few weeks the phone may ring less and the engagements should be worth more.
